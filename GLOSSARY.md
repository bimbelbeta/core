# Bimbelbeta

## Language

**Tryout availability**:
Whether a published tryout is upcoming, open, or expired according to its scheduled start and end times. An unspecified start or end places no time restriction on that side.

**Tryout level**:
A grouping used to discover tryouts: TKA includes SD, SMP, and SMA categories, while UTBK includes the UTBK category.
